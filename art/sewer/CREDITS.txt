Sewer floors compose the CC0 brick strip at native 1×. Three palette files
were made offline from that strip. The walk lip, the solid rectangles, and
the route are unchanged. Valves, shutters, fish, foes, and the other props
are still the old drawing.

walls.png
  Free 16x16 sewer walls by Chris_M_The_Game_Dude.
  https://opengameart.org/content/free-16x16-sewer-stuffz
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal.
  The file is the 176x16 wall strip with the two extra bytes after IEND removed.
  Credit is not required by CC0. It is recorded here anyway.

algae.png
  Free 16x16 sewer algae by Chris_M_The_Game_Dude, same page and license.
  The source strip is kept. moss.png is that strip with the wet-floor palette
  applied offline. It is stamped on floor 1 only.

brick-1.png, brick-2.png, brick-3.png
  The same walls.png strip. Each file maps the three source colors
  (15,2,34), (51,34,107), and (71,51,137) to that floor's brick colors.
  No new pixels were drawn. Floor 1 is the cool sewer, floor 2 the gray
  metro, floor 3 the brown market. The walk lip and the solid rectangles
  are unchanged. Props, enemies, and the route are not claimed done.

Reviewed and not shipped, because they do not fit this side-view palette:
  Reflextions, Market Stand (CC0). Flat kiosk art with saturated red stripes.
  Efilheim, Ever Rogue Tileset (CC0). Top-down tiles, wrong camera.
  Kenney, Pixel Platformer Industrial Expansion (CC0). Outlined props, hazard
  stripes, and generic crates. The pack was not copied into the tree.
  0x72 sewer expansion is paid and was not downloaded.
